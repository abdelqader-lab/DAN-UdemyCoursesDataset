2. Data Cleaning Workflow

Problems identified

  • price contained the string "Free" → converted to 0.
  • content_duration mixed hours, mins & questions → parsed to duration_minutes.
  • published_timestamp as string → parsed to datetime + year/month.
  • Duplicate course_id rows and column-shifted rows dropped.
  • Missing subject values dropped (< 0.1 % of rows).

Result

Clean, typed DataFrame with 3,682 rows × 14 columns, zero nulls in the analytical features.

3. Subject & Level Distribution

Web Development holds the largest share (≈34 %), followed by Business Finance, Musical Instruments, and Graphic Design. Beginner-level courses dominate every subject.

4. Pricing Landscape

Prices cluster tightly between $20 and $75. Courses priced above $150 are rare and don't gain more subscribers — price and popularity are essentially uncorrelated.

5. Popularity Drivers

Reviews correlate with subscribers at r ≈ 0.87, making them a strong proxy for enrolment. Web Development consistently attracts the highest median subscriber count among all four subjects.

6. Time Trend

Publishing volume exploded between 2015–2016, while average price declined — Udemy's marketplace shifted toward mass-market, lower-priced content.
